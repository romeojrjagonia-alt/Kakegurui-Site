About the Series

Kakegurui is a Japanese manga series written by Homura Kawamoto and illustrated by Tōru Naomura. The story takes place at Hyakkaou Private Academy, a prestigious school where the student hierarchy is determined by gambling prowess.

When transfer student Yumeko Jabami arrives, she disrupts the established order with her love for high-stakes gambling and her ability to expose cheaters. Unlike others who gamble for money or power, Yumeko gambles purely for the thrill of it.

The series explores themes of risk, power dynamics, psychological manipulation, and the addictive nature of gambling, all wrapped in a stylish and intense narrative that keeps viewers on the edge of their seats.

Main Characters

Yumeko Jabami

The mysterious transfer student with an insatiable appetite for high-stakes gambling. Her unpredictable nature and genuine love for the thrill of gambling make her a formidable opponent.

Mary Saotome

A skilled gambler who initially opposes Yumeko but later becomes her ally. She's determined to rise through the ranks and overthrow the student council.

Kirari Momobami

The student council president who rules the academy with an iron fist. She's fascinated by Yumeko and sees her as a worthy adversary.

Ryota Suzui

A kind-hearted student who becomes Yumeko's friend and companion. He often serves as the voice of reason in her reckless gambling adventures.

Core Themes

01

Risk & Addiction

The series explores the psychological aspects of gambling addiction and the rush of putting everything on the line.

02

Power Dynamics

Social hierarchy is determined entirely by gambling ability, creating a brutal meritocracy where status is constantly in flux.

03

Deception & Strategy

Characters employ psychological manipulation, cheating, and complex strategies to gain advantages in their games.

04

Equality Through Chance

Gambling serves as the ultimate equalizer where anyone can rise or fall based on skill, luck, and nerve.
